---
title: "Attendance Policy"
---

1. If you are unable to attend class for whatever reason, please email me before the class in question if possible, briefly explaining the reason for your absence, or as soon as possible after the class.

2. The maximum number of permitted absences for the course is 4 - the equivalent of 2 full weeks of classes - regardless of the reason. If you exceed this maximum, you are required to meet with me during my office hours to discuss your status in the course, and you may be recommended to withdraw from the course.

3. An absence is noted as Absent. There is no distinction between Excused and Unexcused absences. 

4. For 2 or more consecutive absences due to illness, a doctor's note is required. 

5. Please arrive for class on time and do not leave before class ends without asking my permission. Arrivals more than 10 minutes late or departures 10 minutes before class ends are noted as Late and 3 Lates count as one Absent.
